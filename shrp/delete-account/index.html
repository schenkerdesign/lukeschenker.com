Delete your SHRP account

SHRP is made by Luke J. Schenker, operating as Schenker Design, Gibsonia, Pennsylvania, USA. Contact: luke@anew.so.

Deleting is permanent. There is no undo, and SHRP does not have account recovery yet, so you will not be able to sign back in afterwards.

Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open SHRP.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your device lock (fingerprint, face, or passcode). If your phone has no lock set, you will get a slide to confirm instead.

If you cannot open the app

If you have lost or replaced your phone, email luke@anew.so and include your SHRP username. We will confirm the request with you, delete the account, and reply when it is done. Email requests are handled within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, display name, photo, gender, country, and timezone
  • All of your screentime data: daily totals, the per-app breakdown, summaries, medals, streak, and percentile
  • Messages you sent and received
  • Your follows and your group memberships
  • Your push notification token and your notification settings
  • The app also wipes its local data from your phone

What we keep

  • Feedback you sent us, unlinked from your account so it is no longer connected to you. We keep it de-identified so we can carry on learning from it.
  • Analytics and crash diagnostics, held by PostHog and Sentry under their own retention periods. These are tied to an anonymous ID and never to your name.
