Luke Schenker Privacy Terms

SHRP support

SHRP is made by Luke J. Schenker, operating as Schenker Design, Gibsonia, Pennsylvania, USA.

Contact

For any question, problem, or request, email luke@anew.so. Include your SHRP username if your question is about your account.

Report a user

If a username, display name, or profile photo is offensive or abusive, open that person's profile in SHRP, tap their username at the top of the screen, then tap Report. Reports reach us right away and we review them as quickly as we can. You can also email luke@anew.so.

Block a user

Open the person's profile in SHRP, tap their username at the top of the screen, then tap Block. Each of you disappears from the other's app, any follows between you are removed, and neither of you can follow or message the other. To unblock, go to Settings, then Account & Discovery, then Your block list. Unblocking does not restore follows.

Your privacy

Read what SHRP collects and how it is used in the SHRP Privacy Policy. You can change who sees your data in Settings.

Delete your account

Go to Settings, then Account & Discovery, then Delete my account and info. More detail, including what is kept, is on the account deletion page.

See also the SHRP Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
